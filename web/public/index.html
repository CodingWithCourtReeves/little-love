Channels

Like Discord, but just for the two of you.

How it works

Three steps, and it’s just the two of you.

  1. Ask for an invite.

    Leave your email on this page. We’re letting couples in a few at a time while we test.

  2. Install it on your iPhone.

    We’ll email you a link through TestFlight, Apple’s app for trying new apps. When you sign up, LittleLove shows you 12 words. Write them down and keep them safe.

  3. Invite your partner.

    LittleLove gives you a link to send them (or a QR code, or four words to read out). Once they open it on their iPhone, you’re connected.

The guarantee

Not even we can read it.

Every message is locked on your phone before it’s sent, and only your partner’s phone has the key. What passes through our servers is scrambled text we have no way to open. That’s what “end-to-end encrypted” means. It isn’t a setting, and it isn’t a promise. It’s how the app is built.

  • No ads. We don’t have anything to sell, including you.
  • No trackers. No outside analytics, no profiling. If the app crashes, the report goes to servers we run ourselves, and only if you turn it on.
  • No AI. Nothing reads your conversation to “improve” anything.

To deliver a message we need to know it was sent, and when. We don’t use that to profile your relationship, target ads, or measure engagement.

Questions

The things people ask us.

Is it free?

Yes. LittleLove is free, with no ads.

What’s a channel?

A chat with its own topic, like #travel or #the-house. It’s always just the two of you, and your partner is added automatically. Channel names aren’t encrypted, but everything you send in them is.

Does my partner need it too?

Yes. LittleLove connects exactly two people, so you’ll both need it on an iPhone. While we’re testing, put your partner’s email in the note at the bottom of this page and we’ll invite you both.

Is it only for iPhone?

For now. Android is on our list, but we don’t have a date yet.

Can I add anyone else?

No. Each account is paired with exactly one partner. That’s the point.

What if I lose my phone?

On a new iPhone, your username and the 12 words you saved at sign-up get you back in. Because only your phones hold the keys, we can’t reset them for you. If you lose your phone and your 12 words, your messages can’t be recovered, by you or by us.

What’s TestFlight?

Apple’s own app for trying apps before they’re in the App Store. You install TestFlight from the App Store, open the invite we email you, and LittleLove installs like any other app.
